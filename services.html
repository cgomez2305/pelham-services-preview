What We Do

Freight solutions built around your lane, not ours.

Three core services carry most of our freight — transloading, dedicated transport and consolidation — backed by local and final-mile capacity across California and Florida.

01 · Transloading

Transloading

Seamless cargo transitions through our warehouse network — cargo moves in on one trailer or container and out on another, without the delay of a full reload cycle.

Who it's for: Shippers moving ocean or rail containers inland, or anyone who needs freight re-sorted between equipment types mid-route.

Facilities: Los Angeles · Jacksonville · Tampa · Orlando · Miami

Request Transloading Rates

  1. Cargo arrives

    Containers or trailers are received at the nearest transloading facility to origin.

  2. Sort & inspect

    Freight is unloaded, checked against manifest, and staged for its next leg.

  3. Reload

    Goods are loaded into the outbound trailer or container matched to the final destination.

  4. Dispatch

    The outbound load moves under the same visibility and tracking as any Pelham shipment.

02 · Dedicated Transport

Dedicated Transport

Coast-to-coast, full-truckload capacity that runs like an extension of your own fleet — team drivers, advanced GPS tracking, and a single point of contact for every load.

Who it's for: Shippers with recurring volume who need predictable capacity instead of competing for space on the spot market.

Coverage: Coast-to-coast, with concentrated capacity between California and Florida.

Request Dedicated Capacity
03 · Freight Consolidation

Freight Consolidation

We combine less-than-truckload shipments from multiple shippers into fuller, more efficient loads — cutting cost-per-unit and empty miles without adding transit risk.

Who it's for: Shippers with volume too small to justify a dedicated truck, but who still need reliable, scheduled service.

Result: Lower cost per shipment, improved handling, and freight that still arrives on schedule.

Request Consolidated Rates

  1. Pool shipments

    Compatible freight from multiple shippers is grouped by lane and timing.

  2. Optimize the load

    We build the trailer to maximize space and minimize handling risk.

  3. Move & monitor

    The consolidated load ships on a fixed schedule with full tracking.

  4. Deconsolidate

    Freight is separated and delivered to each final destination on arrival.
