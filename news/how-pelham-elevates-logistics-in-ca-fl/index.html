How Pelham Services Elevates Logistics in California & Florida

September 27, 2025 · By Pelham Services

Reliability and agility aren't optional in today's supply chain — shippers need to know a load will move on schedule, and that if something changes mid-route, someone with real authority over the truck and the warehouse can actually fix it. That's the problem Pelham Services was built to solve on the California–Florida lane.

An integrated fleet-and-warehouse model

A lot of logistics providers outsource pieces of their network — the trucking to one contractor, the warehousing to another, the final mile to a third. Every handoff between those parties is a place where a shipment can get delayed, mishandled, or lost in a communication gap. Pelham takes a different approach: we own and operate both the transportation assets and the storage/transloading facilities ourselves. Fewer handoffs means tighter control over scheduling, handling, and risk from pickup to delivery.

Pool distribution across high-density corridors

In busy corridors across California and Florida, we consolidate goods from multiple shippers into the same trailer, which improves load factors, cuts down on empty miles, and lowers per-unit cost for everyone sharing the lane. It's the same logic behind our freight consolidation service — smarter loading, not just more trucks.

Local presence, coast-to-coast reach

Our strongholds are Los Angeles, Gardena, and Miami, with additional transloading capacity in Jacksonville, Tampa, and Orlando. That combination gives us a genuine local presence at both ends of the country's busiest coast-to-coast freight lane, backed by the terminal network described on our locations page.
