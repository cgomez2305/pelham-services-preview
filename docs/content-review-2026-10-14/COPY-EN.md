## Full Truckload (FTL)

Excerpt: Exclusive trailer capacity for your shipment between California and Florida. Whether you are moving a full load or need dedicated space, plan a single shipment or a recurring lane with our team.

### Overview — A trailer planned around your load.

Full truckload shipping reserves trailer capacity for one shipper’s freight. It can suit a load that fills the trailer or a shipment that needs exclusive space even when the trailer is not full.

Tell us your pickup and delivery locations, cargo details and preferred dates. We’ll discuss capacity, equipment and handling requirements before you book. For smaller loads that can share space, explore our Less Than Truckload (LTL) service.

### How it works

1. Share the route: Provide pickup and delivery locations, requested dates and whether this is a single shipment or recurring work.
2. Describe the load: Include cargo type, packaging, dimensions, total weight and equipment or handling needs.
3. Confirm the shipment: Review the proposed service, availability, pricing and timing with our team before booking.

### Prepare your quote

Pickup and delivery city/state or ZIP; requested dates; commodity and packaging; dimensions and total weight; equipment or handling needs; frequency if recurring.
CTA: Request an FTL quote

### Questions

**Does my shipment have to fill the trailer?** No. FTL may also be appropriate when a shipment requires exclusive trailer capacity. Share the details so our team can confirm suitability.

**Can I request a one-time shipment?** You can enquire about a single load or a recurring lane. Include your requested dates and locations so we can confirm availability.

**How does FTL differ from LTL?** FTL reserves trailer capacity for one shipper. LTL uses shared space for smaller shipments. The choice depends on your freight, handling needs and schedule.

## Less Than Truckload (LTL)

Existing URL: /services/freight-consolidation/
H1/service label: Less Than Truckload (LTL)
SEO title: LTL Freight Shipping: California–Florida | Pelham Services
Meta description: Shipping less than a full truckload? Explore LTL freight services between California and Florida. Share your pallet count, dimensions and weight for a quote.
Excerpt: Freight shipping for loads that do not need an entire trailer. Our LTL service combines compatible smaller shipments in shared space, with the transport plan based on your cargo, route and requirements.

### Overview — The space your shipment needs.

Less-than-truckload shipping allows smaller shipments to share trailer space with other freight. Pelham’s freight consolidation service brings compatible shipments together rather than requiring every shipper to reserve a full trailer.

Share your pallet or piece count, dimensions, weight and requested dates. Our team will discuss compatibility, handling and the available service. Rates and transit expectations depend on the shipment; they are confirmed as part of the quote.

### How it works

1. Describe the shipment: Send cargo type, pallet or piece count, packaging, dimensions and total weight.
2. Review the requirements: Discuss pickup, delivery, requested dates and any special handling needs.
3. Confirm the shared plan: Review the proposed service and quote before booking.

### Prepare your quote

Pallet or piece count; dimensions and weight; commodity and packaging; pickup/delivery city and state or ZIP; requested dates; handling requirements.
CTA: Request an LTL quote

### Questions

**What does LTL mean?** Less than truckload: a shipment uses part of a trailer and shares capacity with other freight.

**Will LTL always cost less than FTL?** Sharing trailer space can be an option for smaller loads, but pricing depends on size, weight, route and service requirements. Request a quote to compare the appropriate options.

**What if my freight cannot share space?** Tell us about any separation or handling requirements. Our team can discuss whether full truckload service is a better fit.

## Transloading

Existing URL: /services/transloading/
H1/service label: Transloading
SEO title: Transloading in California & Florida | Pelham Services
Meta description: Coordinate freight transfers between containers and trailers with Pelham Services. Share inbound equipment, cargo details and the next destination.
Excerpt: Connect the next leg of your shipment by transferring freight between containers, trailers or other agreed equipment. Plan the inbound arrival, cargo handling and outbound move with our team.

### Overview — New equipment. The next leg of your journey.

Transloading moves cargo from one piece of transport equipment to another, such as from an inbound container to an outbound trailer. The transfer plan needs to account for the cargo, packaging, arrival details and onward destination.

Tell us where the freight is arriving, what equipment it is in and how it needs to move next. Our team will confirm the appropriate location, available handling and operating requirements for your shipment.

### How it works

1. Describe the arrival: Share inbound equipment, expected arrival and requested transfer location.
2. Plan the handling: Provide cargo type, packaging, dimensions, weight and any special requirements.
3. Coordinate the next leg: Confirm outbound equipment, destination and scheduling with our team.

### Prepare your quote

Inbound container or trailer type; commodity and packaging; piece count, dimensions and weight; arrival details; requested transfer location; outbound equipment and destination.
CTA: Request a transloading quote

### Questions

**What is transloading?** Transloading transfers freight between transport equipment so it can continue to the next leg of its journey.

**Is transloading the same as cross-docking?** No. Transloading describes a transfer between equipment; cross-docking describes moving inbound freight to outbound transport with limited storage. Ask our team which handling arrangement is available for your shipment.

**What information should I send?** Include inbound equipment, cargo details, requested location, expected arrival if known, and the outbound destination. The team will confirm documentation and appointment requirements.
