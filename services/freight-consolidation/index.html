Your pallets. Our specialty.

Ship one pallet, a few pallets or another partial load without reserving an entire truck. Pelham collects your freight in Los Angeles and delivers it to businesses in Florida, including transport companies in Miami that may arrange a separate onward journey.

Our service is domestic trucking. We do not provide the international leg or direct port service. For exclusive trailer capacity, compare Full Truckload (FTL).

LTL freight from California to Florida

Pelham specializes in pallet shipping from Los Angeles to Florida. Share trailer space for one pallet, several pallets or another partial load. Provide the actual California pickup address when requesting a quote so our team can confirm arrangements for the origin, rather than assuming every California location has the same pickup plan.

Our Los Angeles facility is in Gardena. We deliver to Florida businesses, including shops, distributors and freight forwarders. Select a service level using the receiving address and timing needs, then request an LTL freight quote.

Pallet delivery across Florida

Los Angeles to Miami and South Florida. Pelham moves pallets to Miami businesses and transport companies. Expedited LTL estimates 3 calendar days from pickup and is limited to Homestead–West Palm Beach. Regular and Economy are also available for this area. For a forwarder, confirm the domestic receiving address and review our Miami delivery coordination guide.

Los Angeles to Orlando. Regular and Economy serve Orlando with estimated transit of 7 and 10 calendar days from pickup. Our Orlando facility is at 9777 Satellite Blvd suite 130. Confirm the shipment’s delivery address with the team; a business delivery and delivery to a Pelham facility are different arrangements.

Los Angeles to Tampa and Jacksonville. Regular and Economy serve these Florida destinations, with estimated transit of 7 and 10 calendar days from pickup. Supply the receiving business address and its requirements. Pelham does not list a company-operated facility in either city.

All transit times are estimates, not guarantees. Our operated facilities are in Gardena / Los Angeles, Miami and Orlando. Destination coverage is broader than our facility network.

Questions before you ship

When does the transit time start?

The estimate starts when your cargo is picked up and is counted in calendar days. Transit times are not guaranteed.

Where is Expedited available?

Expedited has an estimated transit time of 3 calendar days from pickup and serves South Florida from Homestead to West Palm Beach. Regular (7 days) and Economy (10 days) serve all Florida destinations.

Which option is the most economical?

Economy is our lowest-cost LTL service level, with an estimated transit time of 10 calendar days from pickup. Request a quote for your shipment.

Do you ship internationally?

No. Pelham provides domestic trucking. We can deliver to a transport company in Miami; any onward international movement is handled separately by that company.

What affects an LTL quote?

Share pallet or piece count, dimensions, total weight, cargo, pickup and delivery locations, receiving requirements and your preferred service level. The team confirms pricing for your shipment; no fixed rate is implied by a transit estimate.

For the businesses behind every pallet.

Suppliers and manufacturers: coordinate pallet pickup at your Los Angeles business or factory with the receiving business in Florida. Include loaded dimensions, weight and when the goods will be ready. Pallet shipping for suppliers starts with a clear description of the goods and packaging; acceptance and handling are confirmed for the shipment.

Distributors and wholesalers: plan California–Florida freight around replenishment and business receiving requirements. For recurring shipments, share your expected frequency and how pallet counts vary. Commercial freight delivery in Florida is arranged to the agreed business address; discuss each receiving location separately. See our recurring freight planning guide.

Freight forwarders: arrange the Los Angeles–Florida trucking leg and delivery to your receiving facility. Pelham’s service ends at the agreed domestic delivery point; onward international transport is handled separately.
