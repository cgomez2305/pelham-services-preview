Service · Transloading

Transloading services Miami shippers trust — plus Los Angeles, Jacksonville, Tampa and Orlando.

Cargo shifts from one trailer or container to another at our warehouse network, so freight keeps moving without a full reload cycle.

What It Is

Seamless cargo transitions, five facilities deep

Transloading moves freight in on one trailer or container and out on another — cargo is unloaded, inspected and reloaded at a Pelham facility instead of sitting through a full reload cycle. It's how ocean and rail freight gets matched to the right domestic equipment for the next leg of the trip.

Who it's for: Shippers moving ocean or rail containers inland, or anyone who needs freight re-sorted between equipment types mid-route.

Transloading facilities: Miami, Los Angeles, Jacksonville, Tampa and Orlando.

Our Miami facility works closely with the Miami, FL terminal, and freight arriving on the West Coast can move through Los Angeles alongside our Gardena, CA terminal before heading east.

Frequently Asked

Transloading, answered

What is transloading?

Transloading is the process of moving cargo from one mode of transport or type of equipment to another — for example, from an ocean container onto a domestic dry van trailer — at a warehouse facility, without a full unload-and-store cycle in between.

Why transload in Miami?

Miami is one of Pelham's five transloading hubs and sits at the Florida end of our coast-to-coast network, making it a natural point to shift freight arriving by ocean or rail onto outbound trailers headed across the state or back toward California.

Which facilities offer transloading?

Pelham operates transloading facilities in Los Angeles, Jacksonville, Tampa, Orlando and Miami.
