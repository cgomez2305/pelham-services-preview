Frequently Asked

Dedicated transport, answered

What is dedicated transport?

Dedicated transport means a carrier commits specific trucks, trailers and drivers to a single shipper's lane on a recurring basis, instead of that shipper competing for capacity on the open spot market load by load.

Who is dedicated transport for?

It's built for shippers with recurring, predictable volume — typically moving full truckloads between California and Florida — who need consistent capacity and a single point of contact rather than a different carrier every week.

How is dedicated transport different from freight consolidation?

Dedicated transport assigns a full truckload to one shipper's freight; freight consolidation combines smaller, less-than-truckload shipments from multiple shippers into a single load. Shippers with enough volume for a full truck typically choose dedicated transport, while lighter or less frequent volume fits consolidation better.
