=== Pelham Applications ===
Requires at least: 6.4
Requires PHP: 8.0
Stable tag: 1.1.1
License: GPL-2.0-or-later

Digital customer applications, optional credit, transport agreements and separate freight claims. Draw or upload signatures; authorized staff review private encrypted records and generate signed PDFs.

Install alongside Pelham Content and Pelham Modern. Configure the sales recipient in Pelham Applications > Settings and verify WordPress email delivery and WP-Cron. HTTPS and OpenSSL are required for production. Preserve encryption keys and backups. Supply Vision provisioning remains manual.

See INSTALLATION.md for installation, document coverage and operational limits. FPDF 1.8.6 retains its upstream license in vendor/fpdf/license.txt.

Security update: uploaded evidence is quarantined until a configured malware scanner approves it. See SECURITY.md before enabling real claims.

Optional Cloudmersive adapter is disabled by default. Read SCANNER.md for provider approval and server configuration.

Sales document workspace and original-template PDF exports. See DOCUMENTS.md.
