Suppliers shipping to Florida customers

Send palletized orders from your Los Angeles location to retailers, shops and distributors across Florida. Shared LTL capacity lets you ship one pallet or several without paying for a whole truck.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Expedited: about 3 calendar days from pickup to South Florida, Homestead to West Palm Beach.
  • LTL Regular or Economy: about 7 or 10 calendar days to destinations across Florida.
  • Full truckload when an order fills a trailer.

Include in your quote request

  • Pickup address and the date the goods are ready.
  • Receiving business, city and ZIP code.
  • Pallet count, loaded dimensions and weight.
  • Delivery appointment or receiving hours, if any.

Distributors replenishing Florida stock

Keep Florida warehouses and stores supplied from California vendors. Planned replenishment works best when pallet counts and receiving windows are known in advance, so the team can match each shipment to the right service level.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Regular or Economy for planned replenishment where timing allows.
  • LTL Expedited when a South Florida location needs stock sooner.
  • Full truckload when replenishment fills a trailer.

Include in your quote request

  • Expected shipment frequency.
  • Typical and largest pallet count per shipment.
  • Each receiving location and its delivery requirements.
  • Who to contact at delivery.

Manufacturers and factories in California

Move finished goods from your plant or factory to customers in Florida. If freight leaves production unpalletized or needs a transfer between equipment, Pelham can prepare it before the domestic truck move.

Usually the right fit

  • Full truckload for production runs that fill a trailer.
  • LTL for partial orders and smaller customers.
  • Cross-docking and palletizing to prepare freight before trucking.

Include in your quote request

  • Description of the goods and how they are packed.
  • Loading dock and pickup availability at the plant.
  • Load size or pallet dimensions and weight.
  • Whether palletizing or a transfer is needed.

See cross-docking and palletizing and full truckload shipping, or request a manufacturer quote.

Freight forwarders receiving in Miami

Pelham handles the domestic trucking leg: pickup from suppliers in Los Angeles and delivery to your receiving facility in Florida, including Miami. Onward international transport, customs and port arrangements stay with the forwarder.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Expedited to South Florida, about 3 calendar days from pickup.
  • LTL Regular or Economy, or a full truckload, when timing allows.

Include in your quote request

  • Receiving address and the reference numbers to show on arrival.
  • Receiving hours and appointment rules.
  • The shipper contact for each pickup in Los Angeles.
  • Documents or labels required at delivery.

Questions from business shippers

Does Pelham ship internationally?

No. Pelham provides domestic trucking from Los Angeles to Florida. For export, Pelham delivers to the freight forwarder’s domestic facility and the forwarder arranges the international movement.

Which Florida areas do you deliver to?

Expedited LTL serves South Florida from Homestead to West Palm Beach. Regular and Economy LTL serve all Florida destinations. Full truckload destinations are confirmed when quoting. Transit times are estimates from cargo pickup, not guarantees.
