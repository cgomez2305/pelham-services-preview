Suppliers shipping palletized freight to Florida

Ship palletized freight across the LA-to-Florida corridor, directly from your Los Angeles facility to retailers, commercial buyers and distributors throughout Florida. Shared LTL capacity covers 1 to 7 pallets, and each shipment is billed on the greater of its actual weight or its dimensional weight—not on a whole truck.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Expedited: about 3 calendar days from pickup to South Florida, Homestead to West Palm Beach.
  • LTL Regular or Economy: about 7 or 10 calendar days to destinations across Florida.
  • A volume load or full truckload for 8 pallets or more.

What to include in your quote request

  • Pickup info: exact pickup address and cargo ready date.
  • Delivery info: receiving facility name, destination city and ZIP code.
  • LTL specs: total pallet count, individual pallet dimensions (L × W × H), total weight and commodity details.
  • Consignee schedule: delivery appointment requirements or receiving facility hours.

Distributors replenishing Florida distribution networks

Keep your Florida warehouses and retail storefronts consistently stocked from California vendors. Planned replenishment runs most efficiently when pallet counts, cargo dimensions and receiving windows are confirmed upfront, allowing our team to match your shipments to the optimal LTL or FTL service level for speed and cost.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Regular or Economy for planned replenishment of 1 to 7 pallets where timing allows.
  • LTL Expedited when a South Florida location needs stock sooner.
  • A volume load or full truckload when replenishment reaches 8 pallets or more.

What to include in your quote request

  • Shipment volume: estimated shipping frequency (e.g. weekly, bi-weekly or monthly).
  • Pallet quantities: typical order size and maximum pallet count per shipment.
  • Destination details: delivery addresses for all receiving facilities, with site requirements (e.g. liftgate, dock height or appointment windows).
  • Consignee contact: primary contact name, phone number and email for each delivery site.

Manufacturers and factories in California

Transport finished inventory directly from your plant to Florida customers. Whether your goods leave production loose, require palletizing or need transloading between trailers, we handle the dock prep and linehaul move in one seamless process.

Usually the right fit

  • Full Truckload (FTL): dedicated trailer capacity for high-volume production runs and full-capacity releases.
  • Less-Than-Truckload (LTL): flexible, cost-effective shipping of 1 to 7 pallets for partial orders, smaller buyer quantities and split shipments.
  • Dock services and cargo prep: cross-docking, transloading and palletizing to secure cargo before linehaul dispatch.

What to include in your quote request

  • Commodity and packaging details: description of finished goods and primary packaging type (e.g. boxed, crated or loose).
  • Facility and pickup access: plant loading dock availability, staging conditions and pickup operating hours.
  • Freight dimensions and weight: expected shipment size, including total pallet count, pallet dimensions (L × W × H) and total gross weight.
  • Dock handling services: indicate if cargo requires shrink-wrapping, palletizing or transloading before dispatch.

Freight forwarders

We handle the ground transport from Los Angeles origins to Florida receiving locations, including Miami hubs, so you can focus on international logistics. Forwarders retain complete oversight of customs brokerage, drayage and export arrangements.

Usually the right fit

  • LTL Expedited to South Florida, about 3 calendar days from pickup.
  • LTL Regular or Economy, or a full truckload, when timing allows.

What to include in your quote request

  • Receiving facility and reference details: destination terminal address and all required reference numbers (e.g. HAWB, booking #, file # or entry #) for arrival check-in.
  • Receiving schedule and dock rules: operating hours, appointment scheduling protocols and cut-off times.
  • LA origin contact: primary shipper contact details for each California pickup location.
  • Documentation and cargo labeling: required delivery paperwork (e.g. BOL, packing list, dock receipt) and piece or pallet labeling instructions.

Questions from business shippers

Does Pelham ship internationally?

No. Pelham provides domestic trucking from Los Angeles to Florida. For export, Pelham delivers to the freight forwarder’s domestic facility and the forwarder arranges the international movement.

Which Florida areas do you deliver to?

Expedited LTL serves South Florida from Homestead to West Palm Beach. Regular and Economy LTL serve all Florida destinations. Full truckload destinations are confirmed when quoting. Transit times are estimates from cargo pickup, not guarantees.
