About Pelham Services: domestic trucking

Your freight. Los Angeles to Florida.

Pelham Services is a trucking company specializing in less-than-truckload freight from Los Angeles to Florida. We collect cargo from suppliers and deliver it to shops, distributors and other businesses, including transport companies in Miami.

Our work is domestic transportation. When freight is destined for another country, our service ends with delivery to the transport company handling the next leg. We do not provide international shipping, rail transport or direct port service.

Capacity and handling around your shipment.

Choose LTL for shared trailer space or FTL for exclusive truck capacity. Cross-docking and palletizing support the movement of your freight.

Pelham operates facilities in Los Angeles, Orlando and Miami. Contact our team to confirm the appropriate receiving location and arrangements for your shipment.

For the businesses behind every pallet.

Suppliers and factories: coordinate pickup from your Los Angeles shipping location with the receiving business in Florida. Include pallet counts, dimensions, weight and pickup availability.

Distributors: share your delivery address and receiving requirements so our team can plan the domestic truck shipment.

Freight forwarders: arrange the Los Angeles–Florida trucking leg and delivery to your receiving facility. Pelham’s service ends at the agreed domestic delivery point; onward international transport is handled separately.

Before requesting a rate, review what to include in your freight quote. Need help selecting capacity? Compare LTL and FTL.

Business-to-business freight shipping

Pelham’s domestic trucking connects California suppliers and manufacturers with Florida distributors, shops and freight forwarders. For wholesale pallet shipping or replenishment, share the shipment frequency, load details and receiving requirements. We plan the agreed trucking movement, not the goods’ onward international journey.
