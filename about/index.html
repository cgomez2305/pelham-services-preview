Who We Are

About Pelham Services

Pelham Services is run by people who treat freight like it's their own — from dispatch to delivery.

Our Story

Built around one lane, done right.

Pelham Services started with a simple observation: shippers moving freight between California and Florida were stuck choosing between big national carriers with no personal accountability, and small operators without the network to back it up.

We built Pelham to be the alternative — an integrated fleet-and-warehouse operation with terminals in Gardena, CA and Miami, FL, transloading facilities across five cities, and a team that answers the phone when something needs to change.

What Drives Us

Safety, punctuality, transparency

Safety

Every load, every terminal and every driver operates under the same standard — no shortcuts, no exceptions.

Punctuality

We build schedules we can actually hit, then we hit them — because your customers are waiting on ours.

Transparency

Clear pricing, honest timelines, and a straight answer when something goes wrong — not after we're asked twice.
